ENTRY 00125 September 2026Shadowglen, Teldrassil

Leaving Shadowglen

I arrived with nothing worth calling a fortune and I'm leaving Shadowglen at level six with only a few silver still rattling around in my purse. Somehow, that feels right.

I've learned every spell available to me so far. I bought leather gear from the vendor, replaced my dagger, bought a better bow and picked up a four-slot skinning pouch. I haven't been trying to hoard coin for Passage. If I need something and Candei can reasonably afford it, I buy it. The six-gold requirement needs to survive normal life, not a character refusing to spend a copper.

FIELD RECORDLevel 6Cash on hand: roughly 4–6 silverPassage target: 6 goldAll available spells trainedVendor leather, dagger, bow & skinning pouch purchased

I chose Herbalism and Skinning. For now I'm a gatherer. Some herbs are supplies rather than money: Peacebloom, Bruiseweed, Briarthorn, Liferoot, Sungrass and Golden Sansam are being kept because they'll be useful for health potions later. Anything else can be sold to a vendor or put on the Auction House if it's actually worth something.

Already that creates the sort of decision I wanted from this challenge. A herb in my bag isn't automatically vendor trash. It might be medicine later. A skin isn't just profession XP. It might be tomorrow's coin. And the gold in my purse isn't simply a number climbing toward six — it has to pay for actually living here first.

The servers went down for a new beta build just as I was preparing to leave Shadowglen. An annoyingly appropriate place to close the first page.

BETA NOTE

Too early to judge the 6g Passage requirement. The important measurement will be Candei's wealth after Teldrassil's other objectives are complete. So far the economy is encouraging spending, gathering and preparation rather than hoarding.
