ENTRY 00125 September 2026Shadowglen, Teldrassil

Leaving Shadowglen

I arrived with nothing worth calling a fortune and I'm leaving Shadowglen at level six with only a few silver still rattling around in my purse. Somehow, that feels right.

I've learned every spell my trainers can teach me for now. I traded some of what little coin I'd earned for sturdier leather, replaced my dagger, bought a better bow and found myself a small skinning pouch. Six gold stands between me and the road beyond Teldrassil, but I won't reach it by starving myself of everything I need along the way.

FIELD RECORDLevel 6Cash on hand: roughly 4–6 silverPassage target: 6 goldAll available spells trainedVendor leather, dagger, bow & skinning pouch purchased

I've taken up Herbalism and Skinning. The forests provide plenty if I pay attention. I'll keep Peacebloom, Bruiseweed, Briarthorn, Liferoot, Sungrass and Golden Sansam when I find them; one day an alchemist may turn them into medicines that keep me alive. Other herbs can be traded away when coin would serve me better.

It is strange how quickly everything begins to have a purpose. A flower tucked into my pack might become medicine months from now. A hide taken from a beast might buy my next meal, my next arrow, or a warmer piece of armour. Six gold sounds like a fortune tonight, but perhaps that is the point. I do not want simply to pass through this place. I want to leave it ready for whatever waits beyond.

Tonight I leave Shadowglen behind. My purse is light, my pack already smells faintly of herbs and hides, and the rest of Teldrassil waits beyond the trees. It feels like a good place to close the first page.

IN THE MARGIN

Six gold. Right now I have barely a handful of silver. I wonder whether I’ll laugh at that number when I finally leave Teldrassil… or curse whoever decided it was enough.

— Candei ✦
