Begin with nothing.
Build a life in Azeroth.

You are an unknown traveller. No wealthy main funds your journey. No inherited equipment waits in the mail. Every copper is earned, every profession learned and every friendship established. Over time, your Wanderers can become a living network of craftspeople, gatherers and adventurers.

PART I

The Wanderer's Code

These rules apply to every Wanderer, regardless of race, class or profession.

01

A Humble Beginning

Begin fresh at level one. No inherited gold, gear, bags, materials or consumables. Existing alts are strangers until you earn their acquaintance through the Wanderer's Network.

02

The Zonebound Journey

Your current adventure zone is home until its story and passage requirements are complete. Essential class quests, profession training and services may take you elsewhere, but you may not begin progressing the next adventure zone.

03

Every Story Matters

Read or listen to quest dialogue on your first playthrough. Avoid route guides unless genuinely stuck. Know why your Wanderer is helping before racing toward the objective.

04

A Place to Rest

Camp regularly. Cook, craft, prepare supplies and share profession benefits. When practical, end wilderness sessions at camp; inns and settlements are home when civilisation calls.

05

Live Off Land & Town

Quest rewards, loot, gathering, crafting, NPC vendors and legitimate player trades are allowed. Buy what your Wanderer can afford. This is immersive survival, not Ironman.

06

People Before Convenience

Direct player trade is encouraged. Ask in General or Trade, barter, buy materials and commission crafts. Auction House sales are allowed; Auction House purchases are not.

PART II · RAGS TO RICHES

The Passage Requirement

A finished story isn't enough. Your Wanderer must also prove they can survive.

Before permanently progressing into the next adventure zone, personally possess the required wealth. This is a savings milestone—not a fee—so you keep the gold after passage unlocks. Gifts from alts do not count; fair payment for real goods or work does.

PART V

The Nine Class Challenges

Your class doesn't change the Wanderer's rules. It changes how your Wanderer experiences them.

🏹
HUNTER

The Hunter's Bond

Every companion has one life. Tame naturally and name them. If a pet dies, do not revive it. Return to camp or civilisation, release it and say goodbye before bonding with another. No immediate identical replacement.

🌿
DRUID

The Cycle of the Wild

Experience each wilderness ecosystem. Gather, discover wildlife and travel in animal form when appropriate. Before leaving, return one locally gathered natural material to nature.

✨
MAGE

The Travelling Scholar

Discover one piece of knowledge in every zone: spell, recipe, book, scroll, magical object or lore. Never portal into a destination you haven't first reached naturally.

☀️
PRIEST

The Pilgrim's Oath

When you naturally encounter an adventurer in trouble, help when reasonably possible. Heal, buff, resurrect, protect or provide aid without requiring payment.

⚡
SHAMAN

Call of the Elements

Honour Fire through a meaningful foe, Water at natural waters, Earth through gathering or a landmark, and Air from a high or exposed place before leaving.

🔮
WARLOCK

The Price of Power

Investigate demons, curses, cults and forbidden magic when reasonably possible. Before leaving a zone, sacrifice one useful item acquired there. Power has a price.

⚔️
WARRIOR

The Warrior's Arsenal

Defeat a meaningful enemy in every zone using a weapon obtained during that chapter. Train with meaningful new weapon types and keep memorable weapons when storage permits.

🛡️
PALADIN

The Sacred Oath

Attempt rescue, defence and escort quests you encounter. Protect overwhelmed adventurers when reasonably safe. Once you accept an escort, see it through when possible.

🗡️
ROGUE

One Last Score

Before leaving, pull one successful job using Rogue abilities: pickpocket, crack a lock, infiltrate danger or obtain something through stealth.

THE GOLDEN RULE

Play the character.
Not the account.

When the rules don't cover something, ask what makes sense for someone actually living in Azeroth. The Wanderer Challenge isn't about making WoW unnecessarily punishing. It's about making its world, stories, professions and people matter again.
